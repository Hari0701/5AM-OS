5AM-OS

Started as an empty repository in February 2023, on the theory that the
only way to find out how an operating system works is to be forced to
decide what it does next.

Read the source in the order it was written and it is roughly a course:
serial, descriptor tables, interrupts, a keyboard, memory, a heap, a
scheduler, a privilege boundary, a loader, and now a disk.

Nothing here is linked in from a crate you have not read.
